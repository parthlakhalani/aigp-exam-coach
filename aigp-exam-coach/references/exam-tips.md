# AIGP Exam Tips

Check the latest exam format in the IAPP Certification Candidate Handbook on iapp.org.

## Exam format (at time of writing)

- 100 multiple-choice questions: 85 scored, 15 unscored (you will not know which)
- 2 hours 45 minutes
- Scored from 100 to 500; 300 is a pass
- Mostly single-answer, plus some multi-select and ordering questions
- Many questions are scenario-based
- No penalty for wrong answers, so never leave a question blank
- About 1.6 minutes per question

## How to answer

- **Think like a risk manager.** Pick the answer that best reduces AI risk and builds trust.
- **Read the question before the scenario.** Then read the scenario looking for what matters.
- **Watch key words:** NOT, EXCEPT, LEAST, MOST, FIRST, BEST, NEXT.
- **Find the life cycle stage.** Planning, design, data, testing, release, deployment or monitoring. The right action usually fits that stage.
- **Find the role.** Provider, deployer, developer, user, importer or distributor. Duties differ.
- **Answer the actual question.** Two options may be true. Pick the one that directly solves the problem asked.
- **Be wary of absolutes.** "Always", "never" and "only" are often wrong; laws have exceptions.
- **Spot partial truths.** A common wrong answer is true in general but does not fix the root cause.
- **Eliminate first.** Cross out clear wrong answers, then compare the rest.
- **Flag and move on.** If stuck, guess, flag it, and return later.

## Study approach

- Use the official Body of Knowledge as your checklist.
- Learn Domain I early; the other domains build on it.
- Give more time to domains with more questions (III and IV are the largest).
- Plan at least 30 hours of study. Many people use 6 to 8 weeks.
- Mix reading with practice questions and flashcards.
- Review every wrong answer and understand why the right one is better.
- Learn the IAPP AI governance glossary terms.
- Do at least one full timed mock exam.
- Aim for steady scores of about 75% or more on good-quality mocks before booking or sitting the exam.
- Rest in the last 1 to 2 days. Light review only.

## Common traps

- Mixing up provider and deployer duties
- Picking a technical fix when the question asks for a governance step (or the reverse)
- Choosing a later step when the question asks what to do first
- Treating voluntary frameworks (NIST AI RMF, OECD) as binding law
- Assuming any human involvement removes GDPR automated decision rules
- Forgetting that monitoring continues after deployment
